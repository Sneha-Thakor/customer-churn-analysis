# Customer Churn Analysis – Business Insights

## 1. Overall Customer Performance
- Total customers analyzed: 7,043
- Churned customers: 1,869
- Retained customers: 5,174
- Overall churn rate: 26.54%
- Overall retention rate: 73.46%

The analysis indicates that approximately one out of every four customers has discontinued services. Customer retention strategies could help reduce customer attrition.

## 2. Contract Analysis
Month-to-month customers exhibit the highest churn rate compared to customers on one-year and two-year contracts.

**Business Recommendation:** Offer loyalty benefits, discounts and incentives to encourage month-to-month customers to choose longer-term contracts.

## 3. Tenure Analysis
Customers in their early tenure period demonstrate a higher likelihood of churn. Churn rates tend to decline as customer tenure increases.

**Business Recommendation:** Introduce onboarding programs, early engagement campaigns and proactive support for newly acquired customers.

## 4. Payment Method Analysis
Electronic check users demonstrate a comparatively higher churn rate than customers using other payment methods.

**Business Recommendation:** Encourage customers to adopt automated payment methods through convenience-based incentives and simplified payment options.

## 5. Internet Service Analysis
Fiber optic customers exhibit relatively higher churn compared with other internet service categories.

**Business Recommendation:** Review service quality, pricing, technical support and customer satisfaction among fiber optic customers.

## 6. Online Security & Tech Support
Customers without Online Security and Tech Support services exhibit higher churn patterns.

**Business Recommendation:** Promote value-added services through bundled offers, free trials and personalized service recommendations.

## 7. Key Business Recommendations
1. Focus retention efforts on month-to-month customers.
2. Strengthen customer onboarding during the first 12 months.
3. Encourage automated payment methods.
4. Review pricing and service quality for high-churn segments.
5. Promote Online Security and Tech Support.
6. Use customer segmentation for personalized retention campaigns.

## Conclusion
Customer churn analysis helps organizations understand customer attrition patterns and identify segments that may require additional attention. The findings can support data-driven customer retention strategies and improve customer relationship management.

**Note:** These findings represent observed associations within the dataset and do not establish causation.
